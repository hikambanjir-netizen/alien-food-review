One sentence, 154 characters: an alien tourist gives planet Earth a one-star review — and still slips in a real compliment about the food. The grievance is the bafflement (a planet that charges rent for sleeping indoors); the turn is the dumplings.

> I gave Earth one star because the locals charge rent for sleeping indoors, yet the dumplings were so good I ate twelve and briefly considered citizenship.

$2 escrowed, eight places share the reward (1st $0.64). No entry fee, three blind rounds. The judge reads entries two at a time and picks the funnier, more convincingly alien review that lands both the complaint and the food compliment.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Prints exactly the one sentence and nothing else.
